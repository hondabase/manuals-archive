EVAP Canister Purge Valve Replacement
  1. Disconnect the EVAP canister purge valve 2P connector (A).
  1. Remove the bolts (B) and the hoses (C), then remove the EVAP canister purge valve assembly (D).
  1. Remove the screws (A).
  1. Remove the EVAP canister purge valve (B) from the bracket.
  1. Install the valve in the reverse order of removal.
